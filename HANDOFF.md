## 16) Kill a character (mark deceased) + posthumous alerts (follow-up)
- **Fate is a schema-free field marker**, exactly like pictures and possessions: a dead entity stores `fields.death = { chapter: <chapterId>, at: <ISO> }`. Nothing is deleted, so **Kill ⇄ Resurrect is a full toggle** and fantasy resurrections restore the original entity untouched. This deliberately does **not** touch `trashed_at` (that is Trash soft-delete, a separate concept).
- **Client** (`src/entityRelations.ts`): new `getDeath(raw)` → `{ chapter, at } | null`, alongside `getOwnerId`.
- New `src/chapters.ts`: `chapterNumber(c)` (`String(number || order+1)` — same blank-number fallback as the reader) and `chapterLabel(c)`. Shared by the modal and the Bible grid.
- `src/components/EntityPanel.tsx`:

  - The head shows `† Ash` and a red `☠ Died · ch.<n>` meta line when dead; the modal's `chapter` list comes from a new `chapters: Chapter[]` prop.
  - New `deathDraft` state + `kill(chapterId)` / `resurrect()` helpers (both `updateEntity({ fields })`, reload, `onChanged`).
- `src/App.tsx`: passes `chapters` to `EntityPanel` (and `BibleView`).
- `src/views/BibleView.tsx`: dead cards get a `dead` class (subtle `opacity: .78`, back to 1 on hover), a `† ` before the name, and a red `☠ died in ch.<n>` line. New `chapters` prop.
- **Server** (`server/index.mjs`):
  - `parseEntityFields` / `deathChapterFor(entity)` (resolves the death chapter row, or null).
  - `refreshPosthumous(entityId)`: deletes posthumous alerts at/before the death chapter, then **inserts one `posthumous` alert per chapter (after death) where the entity is still mentioned** — flashbacks or continuity slips. ## 23) Entity merge — the Aliases field absorbs a duplicate card
